Vendor Info

Bring your lot booth online

On Lot is the lot online. Make a booth that lives online year round.

Is On Lot right for you?

Handmade / small-batch makers. Artists who already vend on lot or similar events. Vendors who want fans to find them at shows.

On Lot is not for mass-produced resale, dropshipping.

Why set up a booth On Lot?

You will be able to reach fans year round. Focus on supporting our community. Buyers can find items they wished they had bought when they saw it on the lot. Advertising of events & targeted audience.

How to set up a booth On Lot?

  1. Create an Account
  2. Set up your shop
  3. List products
  4. Get found through the shop, event listings & creator spotlight
  5. Ship orders & get paid

What you need to set up your booth.

  • Business Name
  • Photos of products
  • Shows you plan to vend
  • Social Media links
  • Shipping policies
  • Tax ID

Our Policies

What can be sold — handmade / small batch items.

Shipping — vendors ship directly to customers.
